# Drishti v1.2.0 - Better market data and discoverability

This release improves the NEPSE market experience, adds an About page explaining Drishti's purpose and data methodology, and makes the site's public pages easier to discover and share.

## What's included

- **Market tables:** shared sortable data tables across market and company views, with clearer sorting behavior and improved stock data mapping.
- **NEPSE data:** more reliable district and market data handling, including safer geographic lookup and corrected upstream data mapping.
- **About and methodology:** a new overview of Drishti's principles, data coverage and guidance for tracing figures to their sources.
- **Search and sharing:** page-specific metadata, canonical URLs, Open Graph and Twitter cards, structured website data, and generated sitemap and robots routes.
- **Navigation and page details:** clearer page titles and descriptions across key sections, plus small map, district and content refinements.
