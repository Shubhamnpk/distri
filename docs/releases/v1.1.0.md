# Drishti v1.1.0 - Provinces, districts and every local unit

Drishti now covers Nepal from the national map down to individual municipalities and rural municipalities. This release adds a places directory, province and district profiles built from the 2021 census, a global search, and a NEPSE market pulse on the economy page.

## What's included

- **Provinces:** seven province overviews with aggregate population, households, literacy and age structure, a boundary map with popular-place markers, capital weather, and a sortable district table with the full local-unit directory.
- **Districts:** province to district to palika navigation. Each district page shows area, census demographics, a five-day forecast, a locator map, every local unit with its own population, and nearby attractions with images and descriptions.
- **Local units:** individual municipality and rural-municipality pages with population, households, literacy, coordinates, weather and places to visit.
- **Places directory:** searchable index of all 77 districts and 753 local units with Nepali names and areas, filterable by province.
- **Search:** a global search page across districts, local units, companies and projects, with result-type filters and ranked matches.
- **Market pulse:** the economy page now carries the latest NEPSE session (index level, day move, 52-week range, turnover and breadth) from a new `/api/market` endpoint. Index change and percentage are derived from close versus previous close, because the upstream feed publishes unsigned magnitudes.
- **Navigation:** grouped Economy, Places, Projects and Environment menus, a search shortcut in the top bar, and a hamburger layout on narrow screens.

## Data and limitations

Area and Nepali names come from the DataNepal mirror of the national place dataset. Census figures come from BIPAD's 2021 tables, matched to boundary names with transliteration-aware comparison; a local unit with no matching census record shows a dash instead of a figure. Nearby places come from Wikipedia and Wikidata and are limited to a 10 km radius. Weather and forecasts come from Open-Meteo.

## What's next

We will keep filling gaps in census coverage, add more local detail to the district pages, and keep tightening the market and disaster feeds.
